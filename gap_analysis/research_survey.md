# Mathematics Research Workflow Survey

## Purpose

This survey is designed to understand the main difficulties mathematics researchers face when reading papers, managing notation, organizing definitions and theorems, and using AI tools during research.

The results will help shape MathResearch Studio, an AI-assisted research workspace for mathematical literature review and knowledge organization.

## Consent / Introduction

This survey is optional. You may respond anonymously unless you choose to share contact details for a follow-up interview.

Your responses will be used only for research and product design purposes.

## Recommended Survey Strategy

- Keep it to 10 to 15 questions if you want higher completion rates.
- Make most questions multiple choice.
- Use 2 or 3 short-answer questions for richer feedback.
- Send it to MSc students, PhD scholars, professors, and research groups.
- Conduct 5 to 10 follow-up interviews if possible.

## How to Use the Answers

Use the responses to validate these gap areas:

- Notation handling
- Theorem and proof extraction
- Source-grounded QA
- PDF parsing quality
- Note organization
- Dependency tracking
